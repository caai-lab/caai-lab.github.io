01 — Leadership

Founder & Research Mentor — KORA

Gurpreet Singh

Founder of the lab and mentor on every project running inside it. His own work spans multimodal content generation, computer vision and cybersecurity, with published surveys of vision–language foundation models and a review tracing the evolution of AI tooling from 1950 to 2025.

He currently supervises both active projects and co-authors with members rather than for them — every paper on the publications page carries a student name beside his.
