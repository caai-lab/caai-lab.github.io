Join Us

Bring a question. Leave with a paper.

We are always looking for motivated students and researchers. Members join an active project from day one and work it through to a submission.

01 — Open Positions

Recruiting for Fall 2025.

CAAI is accepting new members for the upcoming academic term. Two tracks are open, and both place you on a live project rather than a training exercise.

Track 01

Graduate Research Assistants

PhD and Masters students with a strong background in machine learning, taking ownership of a project's method and evaluation.

Track 02

Undergraduate Research Interns

Juniors and seniors with Python experience and a genuine interest in AI, working alongside a mentor on implementation and experiments.

02 — What we look for

Programming ability and research patience.

  • Python, fluently. You should be comfortable writing and debugging your own code, not only running someone else's notebook.
  • PyTorch or TensorFlow. Enough familiarity to implement a method from a paper and train it.
  • Research aptitude. Reading papers carefully, forming a question, and staying with an experiment when the first three runs fail.
  • Follow-through. Projects run for months. We care more about members who finish than members who start fast.

03 — How to apply

Three steps.

Read the openings

Decide which track fits you, and look through the current projects so you know what you would be joining.

Write to us

Send your background, your programming experience and what you want to work on. Anything you have already built or written helps.

Accepted members confirm their place with the registration fee, then get assigned to a project and a mentor.
