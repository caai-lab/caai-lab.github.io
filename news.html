Breaking · 10 August 2026

Gurpreet Singh’s paper accepted at IEEE International Conference 2026.

Gurpreet Singh, founder and research mentor of CAAI, has had a paper accepted for the International Conference 2026 organised by the IEEE. The proceedings are indexed in Scopus.

The paper is his own work and is published under his university affiliation, not the lab’s — CAAI claims no credit for it. We are marking it here because he is a member of this lab, and because it is the standard of work the people he mentors are being pointed towards.

9 August 2026

Nineteen researchers join the lab.

On 9 August 2026 CAAI welcomed its largest single intake — nineteen new researchers, arriving from journalism, molecular biology, finance, logistics, psychology, law, media and design. Each is being matched to a project, and the fields below are what they came here to work on.

It is the clearest illustration yet of how the lab is meant to work: artificial intelligence is the method, and the questions come from everywhere else.
